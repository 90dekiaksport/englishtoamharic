1. Introduction

Welcome to the English to Amharic Translator. We are committed to protecting your privacy while providing high-quality translation, dictionary, and grammar tools. This policy explains how we handle your information.

2. Information Collection

Personal Data: We do not require you to create an account or provide personal details to use the core translation features. Translation Content: • Text Translation: Processed to provide results either on-device or via secure cloud APIs. • Image Translation: Accesses your camera or gallery only with explicit permission; images are processed to detect English script and are not stored. Device Information: We may collect non-identifying technical data (device model, OS version) to monitor app performance and fix crashes like NullPointerExceptions.

3. Third-Party Services

To provide premium features, we use trusted third-party partners: Google Play Services / AdMob: We use the Advertising ID to show relevant ads under Google’s Privacy Policy.

4. Permissions

The app requests access only when necessary: • Camera/Storage: Required for Image Translation and Dictionary features. • Internet: Required for cloud translations and updated dictionary data.

5. Data Security

We prioritize the security of your data using industry-standard practices to ensure your translation history remains private.

6. Children’s Privacy

Our app is safe for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Contact Us

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our official Telegram channel or the "About" section.
